The patch is easy. The texture is the job.

Ray specializes in cosmetic repairs and matching existing textures. A patch that’s flat but smooth on a textured ceiling still shows. So the texture goes back on, sized and spaced to blend.

  1. RepairCut out the damage, set new drywall, tape and mud it flat.
  2. Read the wallSmooth, orange peel, knockdown, skip trowel or popcorn, and how heavy it is.
  3. MatchSpray or trowel the texture over the patch until the edge disappears.
